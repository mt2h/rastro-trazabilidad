# Surface brief: lot traceability page (index.html)

Scope and visitor mode: Persuade. One static page opened by scanning the QR on a RASTRO bag, mostly on a phone, also on desktop. Each lot has its own address (`#lote-3`, `#lote-3-1`, `#lote-3-5`).

Audience and job: a coffee enthusiast (owns a V60, Chemex, Aeropress, grinder) holding a bag who wants to see where that exact lot came from, learn how to brew it well, and buy another bag at rastrotienda.cl.

Proof and content: real lot data, roast dates, producer, importer and roaster; the route on a map with real photos and logos from the store; base brewing recipes (standard ratios, labeled as base recipes). Route stops after the farm are illustrative and labeled "ubicación por confirmar". No invented claims.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The route is a wake of light, and coffee-brown footprints and falling beans mark it, only on the map. It refuses the dark premium template, the cream artisan template, the folk stripe cloth that was replaced, and the 1990s look.

OWN-WORLD: A warm blush ground with soft fields of purple, fuchsia and yellow light behind everything, espresso-brown ink and coffee-brown prints. The brand fuchsia (#e7037f) and a disc scattered with coffee beans sit behind the matte plum bag. Hairline-bordered frosted glass with layered soft shadows. Thin luminous display type with one heavy phrase. Each lot owns one wake that mixes fuchsia, purple and yellow. Real photographs of brewing tools, cut out from their backgrounds. Buttons, tabs, menu links and headline letters glow as the pointer approaches; nothing follows the pointer.

STORY: The visitor sees the bag shining on the brand disc with real brewing tools around it, reads "Cada café deja un rastro.", picks a lot, watches the wake travel from Caranavi to the cup with footprints and beans, learns which stops are confirmed and which are pending, then picks a brew method, scales the recipe, runs the step timer, and buys the lot.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Blush haze with purple, fuchsia and yellow light. A large thin headline on the left with one heavy phrase, one sentence and two big actions. The bag centered-right on the fuchsia-to-purple disc with a slow shine, sparkles, a floor shadow and a reflection, and the V60, Chemex, Aeropress and kettle photos around it. On a phone the bag leads and the headline sits under it.

FORM: Bioluminescent plankton wake translated from a night sea to a bright blush morning, chosen from the bolder re-roll, seed:91c3629f02c4c550. Raised by one rule: only the current stop is lit (sun yellow), a stage counter stays pinned on the map, and state is a mark (solid versus dotted thread, dashed knot) before it is a color.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
